A little help for your next great spot.

Hey Spot helps photographers and filmmakers remember locations with saved pins, photos, and voice notes.

Common questions

Where are my spots?

Your library is stored on your iPhone. No account is required. Export a backup from the app’s Back Up & Restore menu before deleting the app or changing phones, then use Restore from backup on the new installation.

Why can’t I save my location?

Check location permission for Hey Spot in iPhone Settings. Enable Precise Location and try again where GPS has a clear signal.

How do I add photos or a voice note?

Open a saved spot to rename it, select up to five photos, or record a voice note. Allow microphone access when prompted.
