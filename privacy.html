Hey Spot Privacy Policy

Saved spots and backups

Hey Spot stores your saved coordinates, spot names, photos, and voice notes in its private storage on your iPhone. Hey Spot does not upload your spot library to a developer server. If you export a backup, it contains your saved coordinates and attachments in a readable file. You choose where it goes and who can access it. Your iPhone’s backup settings may also include app data.

Location and weather

Hey Spot uses location permission to save a spot when you ask it to. When live weather is available and location access is allowed, Hey Spot sends rounded coordinates to Apple Weather to get local conditions. If you open a spot in Apple Maps, Hey Spot hands that spot’s coordinates to Apple Maps. You can change location access in iPhone Settings.

Photos and microphone

Hey Spot reads the photos you select through the system photo picker. It uses the microphone when you choose to record a voice note. You can change microphone access in iPhone Settings.

Feedback

Feedback is optional. When you tap Send my idea, Hey Spot sends the text you entered over HTTPS to Formspree, which delivers it to the developer by email. The app does not attach your saved spots, location, photos, voice notes, or an account identifier. Please leave out personal details you do not want to share. An email copy can remain in the developer’s inbox until it is deleted.

Service providers

Apple services process requests needed to provide maps and weather. See Apple’s privacy policy. Formspree processes feedback and support submissions on our behalf and may process technical information for service operation and abuse prevention. Feedback copies may remain in Formspree submission storage and the developer’s email inbox until deleted. Contact us through the support form to request deletion of feedback we hold.

Your choices and deletion

Hey Spot does not require an account or use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking. Delete the app to remove its local data from your iPhone. Manage any exported files or device backups separately. To request deletion of feedback you sent, open More → What’s Missing? and include the approximate submission date and enough detail to locate your original message.

Website support requests

This website’s support form sends your message and optional reply email to Formspree and the developer. Formspree may also process technical information such as an IP address to operate and protect its service. See Formspree’s privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts to this website.

Contact

Hey Spot is developed by Sid. Use the support form for questions or feedback deletion requests. Include the approximate date and enough information to locate the message. App data and exported backups remain under your control.
